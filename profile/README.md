# Edgible's open-source upstream

Tested recipes for running your own apps, such as your photos, your passwords, a wiki, or an AI model, on a machine you control, reachable from anywhere on a real HTTPS address with [Edgible](https://www.edgible.ai). An AI agent can take you from what you need to a running app.

- **[cards.edgible.org](https://cards.edgible.org)**: the catalog. Every card is tested end to end on real Edgible devices, and each need gets an honest answer on whether a machine of your own suits it.
- **[cards](https://github.com/edgible-org/cards)**: the official cards, as agents download them.
- **[card-kit](https://github.com/edgible-org/card-kit)**: the card format, the tools that check and test a card, and the [edgible-cards skill](https://github.com/edgible-org/card-kit/tree/main/skills/edgible-cards) for AI agents.
- **[cards-submissions](https://github.com/edgible-org/cards-submissions)**: add your own app or a card, ask for one, or ask for a card to be tested again.
